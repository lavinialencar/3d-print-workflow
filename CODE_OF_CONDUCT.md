# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

## Our pledge

We want participation to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers. Many people here are learning both 3D printing and the tooling around it.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming a mistake was careless.
- Credit other people's work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory jokes.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as their address, IP, printer access code or account details.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban
anyone whose behavior is harmful.

To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** feature on the item, or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled
privately and fairly.
